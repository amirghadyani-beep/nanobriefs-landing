Perovskite Nanocrystals for Optoelectronic Applications

Kovalenko, Stoumpos, Kanatzidis 2020 🔗 arXiv:2008.01234
Metal halide perovskites have emerged as revolutionary materials for optoelectronic applications due to their exceptional optical properties, defect tolerance, and ease of fabrication. This work investigates cesium lead halide (CsPbX3) nanocrystals synthesized via hot-injection and ligand-assisted reprecipitation methods.
Nano-Metrics: PLQY >90% | FWHM <20nm | Diffusion length >1 μm | Carrier lifetime >100 ns | Mobility >10 cm²/V·s

Graphene-Based Field-Effect Transistor Biosensors

👥 Zhang, Li, Wang, Chen 📅 2021 🔗 arXiv:2106.02683
Graphene-based field-effect transistors (GFETs) have emerged as promising platforms for label-free, real-time biosensing applications. Our device utilizes monolayer graphene synthesized via CVD on copper foil, functionalized with specific antibodies through pyrene-based linker molecules.
Nano-Metrics: LOD: 10 fg/mL | 1000x improvement over ELISA | 95% accuracy vs RT-PCR | Real-time kinetic analysis

Memristive Devices for Neuromorphic Computing

👥 Chen, Kumar, Smith 📅 2022 🔗 arXiv:2203.08765
Neuromorphic computing systems inspired by the human brain represent a paradigm shift from traditional von Neumann architectures. Memristive devices emulate synaptic plasticity and serve as key building blocks. We examine HfO2, Ta2O5, TiO2, and emerging 2D materials.
Nano-Metrics: On/off ratio: 10-10^6 | Endurance: 10^6-10^12 cycles | Retention: >10 years @ 85°C | Switching: <10 ns

Two-Dimensional Materials for Beyond-Silicon Electronics

👥 Novoselov, Geim, Castro Neto 📅 2020 🔗 arXiv:2009.12345
The relentless scaling of silicon-based transistors approaches fundamental physical limits. Two-dimensional semiconductors (MoS2, WS2, WSe2) offer exceptional electrostatic control, high carrier mobility, and mechanical flexibility for post-silicon electronics with gate lengths down to 5 nm.
Nano-Metrics: Mobility: 120 cm²/V·s | Subthreshold swing: 60 mV/dec | On/off ratio: >10^8 | Gate length: 5 nm

Carbon Nanotube Electronics: From Materials to Circuits

👥 Dai, McEuen, Collins 📅 2020 🔗 arXiv:2007.09876
Carbon nanotubes possess exceptional electronic properties including ballistic transport, high current-carrying capacity, and excellent thermal conductivity. We achieve >99.99% semiconducting CNT purity and demonstrate CMOS-compatible processing with thermal budget <400°C.
Nano-Metrics: On-current: 1 mA/μm | Transconductance: 1.5 mS/μm | Subthreshold swing: 70 mV/dec | Frequency: 1 GHz
